# The particles cluster

Everything under `beam.particle.` is the machinery behind Beam's
[particles](/beam/docs/foundations/particles): the declared resources and operations that make up
your API. All five registries are declared by
[`splicewire/laravel-beam`](https://github.com/splicewire/laravel-beam), and most of the time you fill
them without calling them, by putting an attribute on a class and letting Beam's boot-time discovery
register it.

Which one you reach for:

- **Expose rows to list, read and write**: [`beam.particle.resources`](beam.particle.resources),
  usually filled by `#[ParticleResource]`.
- **Add an action such as *close* or *export***: [`beam.particle.operations`](beam.particle.operations),
  usually filled by `#[ParticleOp]`.
- **Mount a resource under another one's URL**: [`beam.particle.relatives`](beam.particle.relatives),
  usually filled by `#[ParticleRelative]`.
- **Add your package's fields to someone else's resource**:
  [`beam.particle.resource-contributions`](beam.particle.resource-contributions), filled from a
  provider.
- **Merge several sources into one paged list**: [`beam.particle.merge`](beam.particle.merge), filled
  from a provider.

## How they fit together

A **resource** is the hub. Operations act on a resource and are keyed under its name. A relative
mounts one resource beneath another. A contribution folds another package's data into a resource's
rows. A merge strategy is what a resource's *composite* backing uses when its rows come from several
sources at once.

## What a duplicate means here

The five don't share one
[**duplicate-key policy**](/beam/docs/foundations/registries#key-policies), and the difference
is the point. Resources, operations, relatives and merge strategies **supersede**: registering a key
that is already taken replaces the entry, which is how your application overrides something a
package ships. Contributions **reject**: two packages claiming the same field on the same resource
is a conflict, so it fails at boot rather than letting one field silently disappear.

## Generators

Three of the five have generators that write a correct declaration with every slot spelled out:

```bash
php artisan splicewire:beam:make:particle-resource
php artisan splicewire:beam:make:particle-op
php artisan splicewire:beam:make:particle-relative
```
